# Getting Started

Welcome to MUSE — an evidence-based impact planning tool built by [Beacon Labs](https://beaconlabs.io). MUSE helps you create logic models backed by research evidence.

## What is MUSE?

MUSE (Modular Stack of Evidence) bridges the gap between research evidence and real-world impact planning. It enables you to:

- **Build visual logic models** — Map out how your program activities lead to measurable impact
- **Leverage AI** — Automatically generate logic models from a simple description of your intervention
- **Link research evidence** — Connect research to support each step of your logic model
- **Save, version, and share** — Keep logic models in a workspace, restore earlier versions, and share them with teammates or by link
- **Trust the evidence** — Every curated evidence entry is stored on IPFS and attested on-chain through EAS, so it can be verified independently

## Who is MUSE For?

MUSE is designed for anyone involved in impact planning and evaluation:

- **NGOs and nonprofits** — Planning evidence-based programs
- **Researchers** — Connecting research findings to practical applications
- **Impact investors** — Evaluating program designs with research backing
- **Policymakers** — Making evidence-informed decisions
- **Digital Public Goods communities** — Tracking and verifying impact

## Access MUSE

### Production

Use the main application for real impact planning:

- **URL**: [https://musecanvas.app](https://musecanvas.app)
- **Use for**: Logic models you intend to keep and share

### Development Environment

A staging environment that receives new features first:

- **URL**: [https://dev.musecanvas.app](https://dev.musecanvas.app)
- **Use for**: Trying out upcoming features. Accounts and saved logic models are separate from production

:::info Language Support
MUSE is available in **English** and **Japanese**. Use the language switcher at the bottom of the sidebar to switch at any time.
:::

:::info When You Need an Account
Browsing evidence and building a logic model by hand on the canvas work without an account. **Signing in with Google or GitHub** is required to generate with AI, to save logic models, and to share them. See [Accounts & Workspaces](../user-guide/accounts-workspaces).
:::

## Quick Start: Your First 5 Minutes

1. **Visit the App** — Go to [musecanvas.app](https://musecanvas.app) (or the [dev app](https://dev.musecanvas.app))
2. **Sign in** — Click "Sign in" at the bottom of the sidebar and continue with Google or GitHub
3. **Navigate to Canvas** — Click "Canvas" in the sidebar
4. **Generate with AI** — Click the "Generate Logic Model" button and describe your intervention
   - Example: *"OSS impact on Ethereum ecosystem"*
   - Example: *"Reducing food insecurity in urban communities"*
5. **Review Your Logic Model** — In about a minute, you'll see:
   - Color-coded cards representing each stage (Activities → Outputs → Outcomes → Impact)
   - Arrows showing causal relationships between stages
   - Green edges indicating evidence-backed connections
6. **Explore Evidence** — Click on green edges to see the research evidence supporting each connection
7. **Save** — Click "Save" in the canvas header to store the logic model in your workspace

![A generated logic model on the canvas, with color-coded nodes flowing left to right and a green, evidence-backed arrow linking two stages.](/img/screenshots/en/canvas-with-evidence.png)

That's it! You've created an evidence-based logic model.
